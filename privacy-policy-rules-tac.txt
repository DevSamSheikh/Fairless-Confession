### ConfessBox Safety & Protection Policy

ConfessBox is a space for honest thoughts, not harmful behavior.
To protect our users and keep the platform safe, every post is monitored by automated protection systems and moderation rules.

By using ConfessBox, you agree to the following:

#### 1. Respect & Non-Abuse Protection

You may not post, share, or imply:

* Harassment, bullying, or personal attacks
* Hate speech against any person or group
* Threats, intimidation, or wishing harm
* Targeted shaming of real individuals

Any abusive content will be automatically blocked or removed.

#### 2. Vulgar & Explicit Content Protection

The platform strictly blocks:

* Sexual, vulgar, or obscene language and No any External Links
* Graphic or disturbing descriptions
* Fetish or explicit fantasies involving real people
* Any inappropriate content involving minors

Such posts are filtered and your account may be restricted.

#### 3. Privacy & Identity Protection

Do NOT reveal:

* Someone’s name, phone number, address, or social ID
* Screenshots of private conversations
* Attempts to expose anonymous users

ConfessBox protects anonymity — attempts to break it will lead to suspension.

#### 4. Spam & Manipulation Protection

You cannot use ConfessBox for:

* Advertising or promotions
* Repeated posting (spam flooding)
* Misleading stories pretending to be real individuals
* Farming reactions or engagement manipulation

#### 5. Safety Enforcement

Our protection system may:

* Hide or remove posts
* Limit posting ability
* Temporarily lock accounts
* Permanently ban repeat violators

Severe violations may be reported according to applicable law.
